Chiikawa Market: when new items drop and how to get them overseas

Chiikawa Market is the official online shop for Chiikawa goods.

When new items appear

  • New items have been released on Fridays at 11:00 JST (for example Sept 18 and Sept 25, 2026). The official account usually announces the next drop in advance.
  • Popular mascots and stickers can sell out quickly. Restocks are published on dated "restock" pages.
  • Some items are made to order (pre-order windows with no quantity limit), which makes them easier to get.

Buying from overseas

Chiikawa Market points overseas customers to an official forwarding service (Gray Parka Service) that ships to other countries. You can also use a general Japanese proxy service.

JP Drops posts new Chiikawa Market items and restocks as soon as they go live, with stock status.
