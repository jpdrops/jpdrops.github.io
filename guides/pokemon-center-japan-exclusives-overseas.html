Pokémon Center Japan exclusives: how overseas fans can get them

Pokémon Center Japan sells many items you can't find anywhere else — plush, stationery, apparel and seasonal collections.

The key rules

  • Pokémon Center Online (Japan) delivers only inside Japan. Overseas fans usually use a Japanese proxy shopping service with a Japanese warehouse address.
  • ID checks (2026): since around August 2026, some Pokémon TCG products and lotteries on Pokémon Center Online require verification with a Japanese My Number card. These items effectively cannot be bought from overseas — and JP Drops does not share ways around this.
  • Popular releases are often sold by lottery rather than first-come-first-served.

What JP Drops tells you in every post

Each Pokémon Center post on JP Drops says whether the item can be bought via proxy, is store-only (look for it later on Japanese resale sites), or requires Japanese ID.
